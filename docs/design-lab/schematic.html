    1Context

    Peppermint develops digital tools that help companies collect and analyse product feedback. I joined as Lead Software Engineer and directed engineering on the user-facing applications that sit at the centre of that feedback process.

    In January 2026 I moved into the role of Fractional Head of Engineering - owning engineering strategy, technical direction and cross-functional alignment.

    Lead Software Engineer · 21 mo Head · 8 mo Apr 2024 Jan 2026 Present

    2Contributions

    2.1Product feedback application

    Directed engineering to create an AI-powered product feedback application that lets clients such as Kuda gather, organise and analyse detailed feedback from testers - the core of Peppermint's offering.

    • Built AI-powered pipelines with LLMs to generate usability study reports.
    • Architected in-app usability tests and surveys, validating early product-market fit.
    $1,000+organic revenue within 3 months of in-app tests & surveys

    50×faster data-intensive operations

    Implemented the wallet - “mints” — integrated with Stripe, streamlining cash flows for both clients and testers.
