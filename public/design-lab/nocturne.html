Overview

Peppermint builds digital tools that help companies collect and analyse product feedback. I joined as Lead Software Engineer and directed the engineering behind the user-facing applications at the heart of that process.

In January 2026 I transitioned to Fractional Head of Engineering — leading engineering strategy, technical direction and cross-functional alignment.

  1. Apr 2024Lead Software Engineer
  2. Jan 2026Fractional Head of Engineering

Key contributions

  • Core product

    Product Feedback Application

    Directed engineering to create an AI-powered product feedback application that lets clients such as Kuda gather, organise and analyse detailed feedback from testers — the core of Peppermint's offering. Built AI-powered pipelines with LLMs to generate usability study reports.

    Architected in-app usability tests and surveys that enabled $1,000+ in organic revenue within 3 months, validating early product-market fit.

  • Growth

    Testers' Application

    Led development of the testers' app, which onboarded 10,000+ testers within its first month. An intuitive interface drove high adoption from day one.

  • Performance

    Performance Optimisation & Scaling

    Made data-intensive operations 50x faster through query optimisations and caching.

  • Payments

    Wallet System

    Implemented the wallet ("mints") system, integrated with Stripe, to streamline cash flows for clients and testers.

  • Leadership

    Technical Leadership

    Led assessment of new engineering hires, maintaining quality and velocity — leading to the successful release of Peppermint V1 after the MVP.

  • Strategy

    Fractional Head of Engineering

    From January 2026: owning engineering strategy, technical direction and cross-functional alignment across the company.

From MVP to V1: an AI feedback product used by companies like Kuda, a testers' app with 10,000+ sign-ups in a month, and a data layer made 50x faster.
