Product Feedback & Scaling Operations

An essay on building the core of a feedback business

Institution
Peppermint
Role
Lead Software Engineer → Fractional Head of Engineering
Period
Apr 2024 – Present
Media
Next.js, NestJS, TypeScript, MongoDB, AWS

Peppermint develops digital tools to help companies collect and analyze product feedback. Starting as Lead Software Engineer, I directed engineering efforts to build user-facing applications that enhanced the product feedback process.

In January 2026, I transitioned to Fractional Head of Engineering — leading engineering strategy, technical direction, and cross-functional alignment.

The product feedback application

I directed engineering to create an AI-powered product feedback application that allowed clients such as Kuda to gather, organize, and analyze detailed feedback from testers. It is the core of Peppermint's offering.

Behind it, we built AI-powered pipelines with LLMs to generate usability study reports — turning raw tester sessions into something a product team can act on.

By architecting in-app usability tests and surveys, the product enabled $1,000+ in organic revenue within 3 months, validating early product-market fit.

Client Kuda Among the companies gathering tester feedback through the application.

The testers' application

I led development of a testers' application that onboarded more than 10,000 testers within the first month. An intuitive interface drove the adoption — the supply side of the marketplace arrived because joining was effortless.

On view tester.runpeppermint.com

Performance & scaling

As usage grew, data-intensive operations became the bottleneck. Through query optimizations and caching, those operations became 50x faster.

Technique Query optimization and caching

The wallet, or “mints”

I implemented the wallet system — “mints” — integrated with Stripe to streamline cash flows for both clients and testers.

Integration Stripe

Technical leadership

I led assessment of new engineering hires, maintaining quality and velocity as the team grew — leading to the successful release of Peppermint V1 after the MVP.

Head of engineering

Since January 2026, as Fractional Head of Engineering, I lead engineering strategy, technical direction, and cross-functional alignment.
